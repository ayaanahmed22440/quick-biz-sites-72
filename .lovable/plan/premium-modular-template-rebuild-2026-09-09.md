# Premium modular template rebuild

## Goal
Replace the seven short, generic local-business templates with polished, long-form websites based on the selected **Modular service system** direction.

## Build
- Rework the shared renderer into a substantial homepage with a premium header, immersive industry-specific hero, trust bar, service modules, about story, process, project gallery, reviews, service-area coverage, quote form, contact details, hours, closing call-to-action, and full footer.
- Keep business details, services, images, reviews, hours, colors, and calls-to-action separate from the layout so every section remains reusable and editable.
- Give split, overlay, and banner presets meaningfully different compositions while retaining one reliable rendering engine.
- Upgrade all seven preset names, descriptions, industry language, calls-to-action, trust proof, and visual personality.
- Preserve lead submission, website editing, live previews, customer imagery, mobile behavior, and published-site compatibility.

## Visual system
- Use the selected premium service-system direction: expressive serif headlines, clean sans-serif body text, strong photography, restrained corners, high contrast, generous spacing, and subtle interaction.
- Let each business brand color drive accents without allowing poor contrast or breaking layout.
- Avoid repetitive card grids, generic SaaS styling, and short placeholder-like sections.

## Validation
- Verify the published cleaning example and admin previews at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Confirm quote submission controls, links, content fallbacks, and all template variants render without errors.
